# 📊 Power BI Dashboard for Retail Insights

This project builds an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze retail sales performance using a transactional dataset. The goal is to transform raw sales data into actionable insights across revenue, products, and regional performance.
________________

## ❓ Business Questions & How We Answered

### **1. Revenue Performance**

**❔How much total revenue is generated? →** 🟢***Displayed using card visual (Total Sales).***

**❔What is the average sales per order? →** 🟢***Card visual (Average Sales).***

**❔How has revenue changed over time? →** 🟢***Line chart of sales by order date.***

**❔Which segments and shipping modes contribute most to revenue? →** 🟢***Bar charts for sales by segment and ship mode.***
___________________

### 2. Product & Customer Insights

**❔Which products generate the highest sales? →** 🟢***Top 10 products by sales (bar chart).***

**❔Who are the top customers? →** 🟢***Top 10 customers by sales (bar chart).***

**❔Which categories and sub-categories perform best? →** 🟢***Sales by category and sub-category (bar charts).***

**❔How many orders are placed per product? →** 🟢***Bar chart of order count by product name.***
_____________

### 3. Regional Performance

**❔Which regions generate the most sales? →** 🟢***Clustered bar chart by region.***

**❔How do states and cities differ in performance? →** 🟢***Column chart (state).***

**❔Which countries generate the highest sales? →** 🟢***Stacked bar chart by country.***

**❔How do sales trends vary by region over time? →** 🟢***Line chart of sales by region.***
_______________

## 🖼 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

**💲Total Revenue →** *Overall sales generated* **($2.26M).**

**📈Highest Total Sale City →** *New York City* **($252.46K).**

**📉Lowest Total Sale City →** *Abilene* **($1.39).**

**🌎Top & Bottom City Orders →** **819** *orders in New York City* **vs** **1** *order in Abilene.*

**🙋🏻‍♀️🙋🏻‍♂️Total Customers → 780** *customers across all cities.*

**🗺️Count of Cities → 529** *unique cities included in dataset.*

**📝Total Orders → 4,922** *orders processed.*

**💰Highest Total Sales Region → West Region ($710.22K).**

**🀄Sales by Region →** *Line chart showing regional performance* **(West, East, Central, South).**
